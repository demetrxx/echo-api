# Landing Structure

## Core Direction

The landing should lead with the real pain:

**Creators do not only struggle with writing. They lose the thread between the moment a thought appears and the moment it could become a strong post.**

Echo is the alternative:

**A calm creator system that turns raw thoughts into clearer strategy, grounded ideas, and stronger posts without replacing the user's voice.**

The page should feel simple, direct, and human. No generic AI-writer language. No content-factory promises. No loud SaaS theater.

## Section Flow

### 1. Hero

File: `1_hero.md`

Purpose:
- make the user feel the core pain immediately
- position Echo as a path from raw thought to strong post
- avoid sounding like another generic AI writing tool

Core idea:

**Your best thoughts should not die in notes.**

### 2. Problem

File: `2_problem.md`

Purpose:
- name the user's lived problem clearly
- show that the issue is not lack of ideas
- make fragmentation feel seen

Core idea:

**You do not lack ideas. You lose the thread.**

Pain points:
- thoughts appear at the wrong time
- notes are scattered across apps
- voice notes, links, screenshots, and half-ideas become dead storage
- writing starts from scratch too often
- AI output feels generic because it has no real context

### 3. Why Current Tools Fail

File: `3_why_current_tools_fail.md`

Purpose:
- explain why existing tools feel insufficient
- create contrast without sounding like a generic competitor teardown

Core idea:

**Tools miss the point.**

They promise help. Then hand the real work back to you.

### 4. Wherever You Need It

File: `4_wherever_you_need_it.md`

Purpose:
- show that Echo is not a rigid linear workflow
- explain that the product helps at the exact point where the user needs support
- make the product feel flexible around the user's real state

Core idea:

**Wherever you need it.**

Echo brings in only what you need, when you need it.

Possible flows:
- thought -> note
- thought -> post
- note -> post later
- notes -> idea -> post
- idea -> post
- draft -> refined post

The user should feel that Echo lets them take exactly what is missing in the moment:
- memory
- clarity
- direction
- idea
- shape
- refinement

## Product Layer Pattern

The "moments where posts die" idea should not be a standalone section here.

Instead, each product layer should show why that layer matters through a simple before/after rhythm:

```txt
Before Echo:
[the real moment where the user feels the pain]

With Echo:
[what this product layer makes possible]
```

Each product section should answer:
- what pain this layer removes
- what Echo adds
- what feels lighter after

This keeps the page moving forward after `Wherever you need it` instead of returning to a separate pain section.

### 5. Capture

File: `5_capture.md`

Purpose:
- show that Echo starts where thoughts actually happen
- make capture feel fast, light, and non-ceremonial
- show that capture removes the first point of loss

Core idea:

**Catch the thought in the form it arrived.**

Inputs:
- text
- voice
- links
- files
- images

Example:

```txt
Before Echo:
The thought shows up on a walk and disappears before you can use it.

With Echo:
Save it as text, voice, link, image, or file - without turning capture into work.
```

### 6. Notes

File: `6_notes.md`

Purpose:
- show that notes are not just storage
- explain how raw material stays usable after capture
- make the bridge from saved thought to future post feel concrete

Core idea:

**Notes should not become dead storage.**

Notes in Echo should feel:
- readable
- usable
- connected to what they could become

Example:

```txt
Before Echo:
You come back to a note and it is still just raw material.

With Echo:
The thought stays readable, usable, and ready to become an idea, angle, or post.
```

### 7. Strategy

File: `7_strategy.md`

Purpose:
- show that Echo gives direction without turning into a planning ritual
- explain why strategy matters before ideation and writing

Core idea:

**Strategy without the heavy planning ritual.**

Strategy in Echo is a live structured understanding of:
- who the user is writing for
- what they are trying to be known for
- what tensions and themes they keep circling
- why they are writing at all

Not:
- a calendar
- a rigid questionnaire
- a content-planning dashboard
- a marketing template

Example:

```txt
Before Echo:
You know you need direction, but strategy feels too big or too generic.

With Echo:
Build clarity through a natural conversation, not a template made for someone else.
```

### 8. Ideas

File: `8_ideas.md`

Purpose:
- show that ideas become stronger because they come from real context
- separate grounded ideation from generic AI idea lists

Core idea:

**Ideas from your real material, not from nowhere.**

Echo should generate angles and post directions from:
- notes
- strategy
- voice
- accumulated context

Example:

```txt
Before Echo:
You ask for ideas and get generic lists.

With Echo:
Ideas come from your notes, direction, and actual context.
```

### 9. Voice

File: `9_voice.md`

Purpose:
- show that voice is a core product layer, not a footnote
- address the fear that AI will flatten the user's style
- make authorship feel protected before writing starts

Core idea:

**Voice keeps it yours.**

Voice is not just tone. It includes:
- rhythm
- sharpness
- density
- phrasing
- what feels natural
- what feels false or embarrassing

Example:

```txt
Before Echo:
AI can make the post cleaner, but it often makes it sound less like you.

With Echo:
Voice keeps the shape, rhythm, and language close to how you actually write.
```

### 10. Writing

File: `10_writing.md`

Purpose:
- make the refine-loop thesis concrete
- position writing as collaboration, not replacement

Core idea:

**Refine the thought until it finally says what you meant.**

Writing in Echo should feel like:
- sharpening
- structuring
- grounding
- articulating
- making the thought publishable without handing over authorship

Supported platforms should be mentioned here as a small supporting point, not as a separate platform-grid section.

Core framing:

**Write for the places where your thinking already lives.**

Possible platforms:
- LinkedIn
- X
- newsletters
- long-form drafts

This should not make Echo feel like a publishing suite. The emphasis is that Echo helps shape the thought before it goes anywhere.

Example:

```txt
Before Echo:
You know what you mean, but the post still does not say it.

With Echo:
Refine the thought until it becomes clear enough to publish.
```

### 11. Product Proof

File: `11_product_proof.md`

Purpose:
- prove the product through transformation instead of fake social proof
- make the user see the before/after clearly
- show that Echo improves real material, not empty prompts

Core idea:

**From messy thought to sharper post.**

This section should show 2-3 concrete transformations:
- raw note -> grounded angle -> refined post
- voice note -> clear thesis -> draft
- saved link + user's comment -> post direction -> publishable version

The point is not to show "AI magic".
The point is to show that Echo preserves the user's original meaning and makes it stronger.

If real user quotes exist later, they can appear here. Until then, product proof is stronger than fake social proof.

### 12. Not A Content Factory

File: `12_not_a_content_factory.md`

Purpose:
- draw a strong product boundary
- make Echo's values explicit
- reject the market fantasy without sounding defensive

Core idea:

**Echo is not here to turn you into a content machine.**

Not optimizing for:
- bulk content
- prompt hacking
- 30 posts in one click
- vanity growth
- generic AI output

Optimizing for:
- clarity
- context
- trust
- identity
- stronger writing
- preserved authorship

### 13. Final CTA

File: `13_final_cta.md`

Purpose:
- bring the page back to the emotional promise
- make the next step feel obvious and low-friction

Core idea:

**Stop losing the thoughts that could become your best posts.**

CTA should point toward starting with capture or joining early access, depending on the actual product state.

5. `5_capture.md`
6. `6_notes.md`
7. `9_voice.md`
8. `10_writing.md`
9. `11_product_proof.md`
