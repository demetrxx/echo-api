# Ideas

## Working Draft

### Heading

**Ideas from what you already have**

### Subheading

No random list of topics. No empty prompt magic.

### Before Echo

You ask for ideas and get something generic.

It may be clean.  
It may be useful in theory.  
But it could belong to anyone.

### With Echo

Echo finds angles from your actual material:
- notes
- strategy
- voice
- saved thoughts
- things you keep returning to

The idea feels grounded because it did not come from nowhere.

### Short UI Version

```txt
Better ideas come from better context.
Echo uses yours.
```

### Notes

This section should make ideation feel earned.

The promise is not "generate more ideas".

The promise is:

**Find the stronger direction already hiding in your material.**
