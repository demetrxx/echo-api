# Why Current Tools Fail

## Locked Copy

### Heading

**Tools miss the point**

### Subheading

They promise help. Then hand the real work back to you.

### Notes

All your note apps store the thought.

They don't help it become anything.

### AI Writers

ChatGPT can write.

But it doesn't know what you mean or how you sound, so it gives you generic bullshit.

### Calendars And Streaks

Calendars and streaks promise consistency.

But they turn writing into debt.
Miss once, feel guilty, stop writing at all.

### Strategy Templates

You need direction and clarity.

But strategy feels like something for those with teams and budgets.
And most advice online gives you a template that was never built around you.

### Closing

It doesn't have to be like that.
