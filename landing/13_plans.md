# Plans

## Working Draft

This section should be simple and quiet.

Not a heavy SaaS pricing table.
Not a wall of feature limits.

The goal is to show that people can choose how much help they want.

### Heading

**Choose how much help you want**

### Subheading

Start small, or give Echo more of the work around your thoughts.

### Layout

Three plans in a clean row:

1. A light plan for capturing and shaping occasional thoughts.
2. A main plan for people who write regularly and want the full system.
3. A higher plan for people who want more volume, context, and support.

### Notes

Each plan should feel like a level of support, not a bundle of random features.

Avoid making the section feel like:
- a spreadsheet
- a productivity app pricing grid
- a pressure tactic
- a list of restrictions

The emotional logic:

```txt
You do not need to become a content machine.
Choose the amount of help that matches how you write.
```
