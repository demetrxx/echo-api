# Strategy

## Working Draft

### Heading

**Direction without the ritual**

### Subheading

Strategy should not feel like a template made for someone else.

### Before Echo

You know you need direction and clarity.

But strategy feels too big:
- pillars
- buckets
- positioning exercises
- audience worksheets
- advice built for people with teams and budgets

It makes writing feel heavier before it makes anything clearer.

### With Echo

Find your direction through a natural conversation.

No premature advice. First, we understand:
- what you want
- why you write
- what you care about
- where you want to grow

Then Echo helps you find your own way there.

Ideas get sharper, angles get clearer, and your posts start to add up.

### Short UI Version

```txt
No heavy strategy ritual.
Just a clearer sense of what you are trying to say, to whom, and why.
```

### Notes

This section should not make Echo sound like a marketing strategy product.

Strategy is valuable because it makes the rest easier:
- better ideas
- clearer angles
- less random writing
- more continuity across posts
